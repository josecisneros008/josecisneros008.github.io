Technologies: Windows Autopilot, Microsoft Intune, Entra ID, Device-Based Licensing
Task & Context: Implemented a standardized, centrally managed pilot for shared Windows computers used by students and instructors at Young Single Adult (YSA) Institutes of Religion.
Challenges Faced: Because multiple rotating students use the same machines, we had to ensure user privacy, block unauthorized installs, and configure Office Device-Based Licensing without requiring personal logins.

3. Multi-Country IT Asset Inventory Reconciliation

Technologies: ServiceNow ITAM, Microsoft Intune, Entra ID, Asset Lifecycle
Task & Context: Audited and reconciled computer hardware discrepancies across ServiceNow, Intune, and Autopilot for Argentina, Chile, Paraguay, and Uruguay.
Challenges Faced: Years of reassignments and cross-border remote work created "ghost records" and mismatched serial numbers, making it difficult to verify device locations.

Results Achieved: Corrected multi-assignment discrepancies, ensured accurate asset audits, and achieved compliance with Church hardware policies.
