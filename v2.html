Week 4 Project Refinement: Mobile QR Asset Scanner Refined Deliverable

Computer Lifecycle Automation (Power Apps & Dataverse)

The Technical Challenge:
Managing device replacements was previously frustrating for field technicians due to slow, manual data entry of long alphanumeric serial numbers, delivery dates, usernames, and decommissioning statuses. Managers also lacked clear visibility into regional inventory. Working closely with my Project Manager, I redesigned manual records into an integrated Power Apps solution.

The Technical Solution:
We integrated a mobile camera QR / Barcode scanner feature into Power Apps. Field technicians can now instantly scan the manufacturer serial numbers on laptops. The system automatically queries Dataverse, auto-populating device specifications, delivery dates, and usernames with pre-selected statuses, completely eliminating typing errors.

Ethical Considerations & Leadership Impact:

  • Respect for Coworkers (Dignity of Work): Reducing manual and repetitive typing relieved administrative stress for fellow technicians, allowing them to focus on real user support rather than tedious paperwork.
  • Honest Stewardship (Luke 16:10): The mobile QR scan physically verifies on-site that a computer exists before replacement or surplus approval, eliminating ghost assets and human mistakes.
  • Transparency for Management: Built a real-time reporting dashboard providing leadership with instant visibility into asset lifecycles, enabling fair, data-driven decisions on hardware renewals.
Documented Impact: Eliminated serial number typing errors, reduced technician device provisioning time by over 50%, and gave management immediate, audit-ready reports.
